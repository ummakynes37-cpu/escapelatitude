Who's behind this

We're a Pensacola couple — both correctional officers with the State of Florida, 26 years in each. He retires December 31, 2026; she follows on January 1, 2028. escAPElatitude is what comes next: a YouTube channel, this site, and a small merch shop, built a few hours a week in the open.

No hype and no highlight reel. If a trip costs more than planned or a system doesn't work, that's what gets covered — the numbers included.

Travel Systems
